# Intelligent Customer Segmentation & Online Shopping Behaviour Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Intelligent Customer Segmentation & Online Shopping Behaviour Dashboard** is a Power BI project developed to analyse customer demographics, online shopping habits, purchasing preferences, and factors influencing buying decisions.

The project uses survey responses from 101 customers to understand consumer shopping behaviour and identify useful patterns through interactive data visualizations.

The dashboard presents customer information in an organized and interactive format, helping users explore shopping trends, customer preferences, satisfaction levels, and personalized product recommendation interests.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyse customer demographics based on age, gender, and occupation.
- Understand online shopping frequency and monthly spending patterns.
- Identify popular product categories and shopping platforms.
- Examine factors influencing customer purchasing decisions.
- Evaluate customer satisfaction and platform recommendation preferences.
- Understand the influence of discounts and customer reviews.
- Analyse customer interest in personalized product recommendations.

## 🖥️ Dashboard Preview

### Dashboard Sections

1. **Overview:** Displays key performance indicators and overall shopping behaviour.
2. **Demographics:** Analyses customer distribution based on age, gender, and occupation.
3. **Shopping Behaviour:** Explores shopping frequency, monthly spending, and preferred platforms.
4. **Purchase Behaviour:** Examines product categories and factors influencing purchasing decisions.
5. **Preferences:** Analyses discount influence, customer reviews, and personalized recommendations.
6. **Insights:** Presents meaningful observations derived from customer survey data.

## 🔍 Key Insights

- The 18–25 age group represents the largest customer segment, with 79 responses.
- Female customers account for 64.4% of the survey participants.
- Students represent 64.4% of the total respondents.
- Clothing is the most frequently selected individual product category.
- 58 respondents spend less than ₹1,000 per month on online shopping.
- Occasional shopping is the most common shopping frequency.
- Customer satisfaction averages 3.6 out of 5.
- 50 customers expressed interest in personalized product recommendations, while 36 were unsure.
